Science, sources & limitations

This app does not use the repository's Earth Engine models. NDVI = (B08−B04)/(B08+B04); NDMI = (B08−B11)/(B08+B11); NDWI = (B03−B08)/(B03+B08). Dry-season greenness and moisture can help flag evergreen woody cover in Afar and Baringo, but irrigated crops, native trees and other species confound it.

Sentinel-2 L2A bands are used as delivered by the tile service; processing-baseline offsets and cross-date radiometric differences can affect these indices. No bespoke reflectance harmonization is applied. PNG values have ~0.0078 index quantization. B11 is natively 20 m; sampling is coarser still. SCL excludes cloud, shadow, snow, invalid and unclassified pixels, but residual cloud and mixed pixels remain. Scene-wide cloud percentage is not plot quality.

Choose comparable seasonal windows. Indicators do not establish causation, invasion area, species identity, clearing success, carbon benefits or legal compliance. No field labels, trained classifier or accuracy assessment is included.

Regrowth or recovery?

Evergreen woody cover can retain a high NDVI floor with low seasonal amplitude. Grassland recovery is more seasonal. These are phenology indicators, not species identification.

One separate STAC search per month; the least-cloudy scene intersecting the center is used. At least 5 clear months per sampled location spanning at least 240 days are required. Missing months are never filled with invented values.

Uses the baseline sampling extent if available, otherwise the current viewport. No clearing history is inferred: compare with a documented treatment date in the field.
Rules & limitations

Woody: minimum NDVI ≥ 0.28 and amplitude ≤ 0.28. Grass: minimum < 0.22 and amplitude > 0.25. Otherwise inconclusive. A single observation per month may miss seasonal extremes; missing months can bias the minimum and amplitude. Low amplitude is not uniquely Prosopis. These fixed thresholds require local validation.

Relative weights

First screen the current extent to map candidate seed sources.
What contributes?

Distance to nearest mapped candidate: exp(−distance/1500 m). Moisture: NDMI scaled from −0.2 to 0.5. Terrain: flatness, 1 − slope/20°, clipped to 0–1. Fire: any valid MODIS burn date during the last 24 completed calendar months, scored 1, otherwise 0. These transforms and weights are assumptions, not fitted relationships. Fire effects are context-dependent; set its weight to zero if inappropriate.

The grid uses the screened viewport, not a newly panned area: re-screen A to change the analysis extent. DEM slopes use neighbouring elevations ~300 m apart and adaptive PNG rescaling; elevation quantization is reported after analysis. Flatness below that precision is unresolved. MODIS is ~500 m, not stand scale. Fire is sampled at cell centers, not complete cell burn fractions. No burn observation is not evidence of no fire. Sources outside the mapped extent are unknown. Missing required moisture or terrain leaves a cell unscored. No MODIS scenes means the fire term is zero everywhere, explicitly reported.

Land use and climate are omitted: no reliable free browser source is integrated at this resolution. ESA WorldCover on Planetary Computer is a legitimate future land-use addition, not used here.

Interpretation, not a benefit estimate

Scores are relative within the current extent, not absolute. Grazing is the share of sampled land within 1 km classified as seasonal grass (minimum 3 seasonally usable samples), not predicted yield. Water access is exp(−distance/1000 m) to a sampled NDWI ≥ 0.1 location; no detected water scores zero, not proof of no access. Slope/20° is an erosion-concern proxy, not measured erosion. Clearing steep terrain can worsen erosion without restoration. Density is candidate sample count normalized to the largest cluster, not canopy cover. No costs, tenure, livestock data or biodiversity effects are modeled.

Automated MRV quality control

Screen claimed clearing with before/after NDVI at the exact supplied coordinate. No random demonstration records are treated as real claims.

Header optional; quoted CSV fields supported. Up to 30 records per run. Only clearing/removal claims are assessed; other activities are inconclusive.

“Not verified” means the satellite record does not support the claim — it is not proof the work was not done. Cloud, scene gaps and small plots cause false negatives. This is a screening flag for follow-up.

“Verified” only means a spectral drop supports the claim, not that clearing is proven. Drought, seasonality, fire and agricultural changes also lower NDVI. The least-cloudy scene before and after the date is used; local SCL quality is checked. No usable pair → inconclusive. Data, scene IDs, dates, cloud percentages, thresholds and reasons are included in the audit.
